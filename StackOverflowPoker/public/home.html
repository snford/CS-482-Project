How Seven-Card Stud works

1 · Ante up

Everyone puts a small ante in the pot before any cards are dealt.

2 · Seven cards

You get 3 cards face down and 4 face up, with betting after each new card.

3 · Best five win

At showdown, the best five-card poker hand takes the pot.
